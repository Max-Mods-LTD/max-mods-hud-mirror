MAX MODS iDrive Apps — HUD Mirror 0.5.1

0. Missing Connect error? This 0.5.1 car-only fix makes all helpers available.
   Keep Android 0.5.0 if already installed. No phone update needed.

1. Car Store > Settings > Check now. Update Max Mods HUD Mirror to 0.5.1.
   Its dependencies include Connect 0.2.0 and GPU Video 0.1.0.
2. Install downloads/Max_Mods_HUD_Mirror_Android_v0.5.0.apk over the phone app.
3. Park in P. Keep your working Bluetooth / Android Auto sound connection.
4. Connect the phone to BMW Wi-Fi. Phone > Start sharing > Allow.
5. Car > Pair with QR. Phone > Scan car QR. The selected display starts.
6. Later sessions: start sharing on the linked phone; no second Start needed.
   Android still requires its capture permission. A changed phone IP needs pairing.
7. Car > Display > destination box: HUD / instrument cluster / iDrive screen.
   Only one runs at once. BMW menus also choose Compact / Full and video options.
8. Keep mirroring on other pages: On lets HUD/cluster continue while you use
   Android Auto on the main screen. iDrive-screen video uses the mirror app page.
9. Phone > Display > Choose video quality: Sharp 30 / Smooth 60 fps target.
   Stop sharing before changing it. Optional Display60 enables the shared
   renderer's 60 fps clock; install while other display apps are stopped.
10. Automatic GPU mode uses a separate instance of the already-installed,
    compatible Waze decoder. Missing/failed hardware falls back to JPEG.
    The adapter does not install Waze or modify its firmware/AA patches.
11. Force close exits only this app and its output. Reopen to reconnect.
12. Display > Video and background options > Connection diagnostics shows
    decoder/fps/signal ages/last issue and offers Reconnect linked phone.
    Updates in both apps lists this version's changes.

GPU decoding, QR on the BMW network, cluster slot ownership and actual display
frame rate still require a car test. Host link results: 29.0 / 57.5 fps.
A 60 Hz clock is not a guarantee of 60 fps. Video remains Park-only.

QR fails? Show a fresh QR, check Connect 0.2.0 and use the same BMW Wi-Fi.
The new QR offers matching local addresses and waits for helper startup.
Pair manually remains available. If Automatic GPU fails, choose JPEG compatibility.
Use Forget linked car on the phone to reset its reconnect credential.
