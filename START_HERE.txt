MAX MODS iDrive Apps — HUD Mirror 0.4.0

1. Car Store > Settings > Check now. Install Max Mods HUD Mirror 0.4.0.
2. Install downloads/Max_Mods_HUD_Mirror_Android_v0.4.0.apk over the old phone app.
3. Install Max Mods Connect in the car Store for QR pairing.
4. Install optional Max Mods Display60 if you want the 60 fps renderer clock.
5. Park in P. Keep your working Bluetooth/Android Auto sound connection.
6. Connect the phone to BMW Wi-Fi. Open the phone app > Start sharing > Allow.
7. Car app > Pair with QR. Phone app > Scan car QR. Then Start mirror in the car.
8. Car app > Display: choose HUD / instrument cluster and Compact / Full screen.
   Keep mirroring on other pages: On lets you leave this iDrive page.
9. Phone app > Display > Choose video quality: Sharp 30 or Smooth 60 fps target.
   Stop sharing before changing that profile, then start and pair again.
10. Updates inside both apps explains the changes. Sessions last 10 minutes.

Actual HUD/cluster fps, QR networking and simultaneous Android Auto need a car
check. About 58 fps was received in the host Smooth test; that is not a measured
projector rate. Native renderer motion packing also limits picture quality.
Video remains Park-only. Stop and vehicle checks apply on all iDrive pages.

QR fails? Try a fresh code, confirm both devices share the same BMW network and
Max Mods Connect is installed. Pair manually remains available.
