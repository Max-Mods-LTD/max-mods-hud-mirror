MAX MODS HUD MIRROR — CAR 0.2.0 / ANDROID 0.2.0

This is an experimental windscreen HUD mirroring app for your iDrive 7 M4.
The earlier build displayed on Max's HUD. This streaming update still
needs a new car test; 30 fps is the target, not a measured projector rate.

The Android installer is in this repository's downloads folder:
  downloads/Max_Mods_HUD_Mirror_Android_v0.2.0.apk

The car folder holds the car app; android holds the phone app's source.
In the car's Store, add this source and choose main:
  Max-Mods-LTD/max-mods-hud-mirror

UPDATE BOTH APPS: Store > Settings > Check now, then install when the
Available version says 0.2.0. Install the new phone APK over the old app.
Stop sharing and pair again after the updates.

First test:
1. Add the source in the car's Store and install Max Mods HUD Mirror.
2. Keep the car stationary in P and turn the HUD on.
3. Open HUD Mirror in iDrive and press Test HUD (8 seconds).
4. Only if the test pattern appears on the windscreen, continue.
5. Install the Android APK and join the BMW Wi-Fi hotspot.
6. Start sharing on the phone and approve Android's screen-share prompt.
7. Enter the phone's address/code using Pair phone in the car app.
8. Press Start mirror. Keep the car app's page open.
9. Rotate the phone to landscape for the wide view. Check all four edges.
10. HUD size toggles Compact / Full screen. Compact is the default.
    Check BMW's speed and sign remain visible; this is not yet verified.

Scroll down to What's new. Use Previous change / Next change to read the
release notes with the touchscreen or iDrive controller.

Stop mirror on the car and Stop sharing on the phone when finished.
The prototype supports display only, targets 30 frames/second, no audio or
phone control. It requires fresh live speed and Park readings and stops
when those readings are unsafe, unavailable or stale.

See README.md for full instructions and troubleshooting.
